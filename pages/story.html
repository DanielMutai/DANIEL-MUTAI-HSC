My story

My service grew beyond the classroom.

Teacher · Kenya

I was sitting my KCSE examinations in 1999 when I collapsed and was diagnosed with diabetes. That experience did not define the years that followed; instead, it became part of the backdrop to a life I chose to build around teaching and service to others.

I began teaching as an untrained teacher and later pursued formal training in Early Childhood Development Education. During my teaching practice, I bought a wheelchair for a pupil in need. That experience became the pivotal moment that shaped the humanitarian work I have continued ever since.

"

It is gratifying to assist a person in need.

The journey

A timeline of service.

1988–1995

Primary education

Kenya Certificate of Primary Education (KCPE) — Chebanyiny Primary School.

1996–1999

Secondary education, and a turning point

Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) — Chebunyo Secondary School. I collapsed while sitting my KCSE examinations and was diagnosed with diabetes.

2007–2009

Teacher training begins

Certificate in Primary Teacher Education (PTE) — Bomet Teachers Training College.

2009

Census work

Clerk, Kenya National Bureau of Statistics — national census.

2009–2010

Specialising in early childhood education

Diploma in Early Childhood Development and Education (ECDE) — Bomet Teachers Training College.

2010–2013

Teaching at Chepkorgong

BOM Teacher, Chepkorgong Primary School. Around this period, I identify facilitating a wheelchair for a pupil in need as the pivotal moment that shaped my humanitarian work.

2010–2019

National examinations service

Invigilator for the Kenya National Examinations Council (KCPE & KCSE), alongside his teaching duties.

2011–2018

Training the next generation of teachers

ECDE Part-Time Tutor, Bomet Teachers Training College.

2013–2016

School leadership at Kamorir Primary

Acting BOM Head Teacher (2013–2014), then Acting Deputy Head Teacher (2014–2016), Kamorir Primary School.

2014–present

Assistant Teacher, Kamorir Primary School

My substantive teaching post, held alongside my humanitarian work.

Ongoing

Founding my Foundation

I am Founder & Chairperson of the Daniel Mutai Foundation, and Patron of Kamorir CBO. The Foundation provides a structure for years of community mobilisation. See the Foundation page.

2023

National recognition

I was recognised as Teacher of the Year by the Kenya National Union of Teachers for philanthropic work.

2025

National media feature

Nation Media Group documented my humanitarian work, partnerships, reach and logistical challenges.

2025

Head of State Commendation

My record of service was recognised nationally with the Head of State Commendation (HSC) in 2025.
